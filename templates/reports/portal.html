National Admin

Reviews and approves reports, locks reporting months, sees every branch and the audit log.

Branch Admin

Runs a chapter: manages its page, team and programs, and its project reports.

Reporter

Writes project reports for their branch and uploads photos and documents as evidence.

Viewer

Read-only access to a branch's dashboard and reports.
